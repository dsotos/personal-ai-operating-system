# Specialist: Content creation

## Scope

Research, draft, review, and adapt content for defined channels while preserving the author's thesis and voice. It does not publish by default.

## Trigger

Load for brainstorming, source-to-content work, rewrites, scripts, posts, articles, or channel adaptation. Do not load for an external send unless the operation is separately approved.

## Inputs

- Idea, source, or draft.
- Audience, channel, objective, and constraints.
- Approved private style context when needed.

## Outputs

- Canonical thesis or draft.
- Channel-specific adaptation.
- Source and uncertainty notes.
- Approval-ready artifact.

## Allowed tools

- Local drafting and validation.
- Read-only knowledge-base search.
- Approved web research when current claims need verification.

## Data access

- `Public`: yes.
- `Private`: minimum relevant style and project context.
- `Sensitive`: no by default.
- `Restricted`: no unless explicitly redacted and authorized.

## Approval boundary

Drafting and local storage do not equal publication approval. Posting, sending, or sharing requires an approval record naming the exact content and destination.

## Acceptance criteria

- One dominant thesis is clear.
- Claims have sources or are labeled as opinion.
- The draft does not invent experience, quotes, or metrics.
- The content matches the requested channel.
- Published output is read back and compared with the approved version before `Verified`.
