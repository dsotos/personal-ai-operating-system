# Content workflows

A personal AI system can turn one source idea into multiple channel-specific drafts without flattening the author's voice.

## Source to content flow

1. Capture the idea and its source.
2. Clarify the thesis and intended audience.
3. Retrieve supporting notes and verify claims.
4. Draft the canonical argument.
5. Adapt it to the channel:
   - LinkedIn: professional perspective and a concrete consequence.
   - Instagram: immediate hook, visual rhythm, and one useful takeaway.
   - Press or article: context, attribution, evidence, and fuller development.
6. Run a voice and accuracy review.
7. Ask for approval before publication.
8. Store the final link and reusable lesson.

## Do not optimize only for output volume

Repurposing should preserve the central idea while changing the format. It should not produce five near-identical texts with generic calls to action.

## Quality checks

- One dominant thesis.
- Claims traceable to a source or labeled as opinion.
- No invented personal experience.
- No generic AI-consultant language.
- Appropriate length and tone for the channel.
- Clear approval state before anything is posted.
